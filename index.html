About

I spent years in healthcare IT before I started anything. That is where Carex came from in 2017, and Anura after it. Figgy came from a house full of kids and a couch that could not survive them.

I went to the University of Pennsylvania and I am a member of YPO. I live outside Madison with my family.

Most of what I write and talk about is the unglamorous part of building companies. Hiring, money, staffing margins, what a factory move does to your quarter, and how any of it fits around six children.
